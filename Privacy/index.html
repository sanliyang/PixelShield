隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

1. 数据存储与处理 / Data Storage & Processing

本应用（PixelShield）所有图片处理、加密、解密及混淆操作均在您的设备本地（Local Device）完成。我们不拥有服务器，不会上传、存储或分析您的任何原始图片或处理后的图片。

All image processing, encryption, decryption, and obfuscation operations in PixelShield are performed locally on your device. We do not own servers and will never upload, store, or analyze any of your original or processed photos.

2. 网络权限 / Network Access

本应用仅在进行 App Store 内购（StoreKit）验证时需要网络连接。除此以外，应用的核心功能完全离线运行。

This app only requires a network connection when verifying App Store In-App Purchases (StoreKit). Other than that, the core functions of the app run completely offline.

3. 密钥安全 / Key Security

您的加密密钥（密码）仅存储在您的脑海中。应用不会保存、上传或备份您的密码。一旦遗忘，数据将无法找回，请务必妥善保管。

Your encryption key (password) is stored only in your mind. The app does not save, upload, or backup your password. Once lost, data cannot be recovered. Please keep it safe.

4. 相册权限 / Photo Library Access

我们需要访问您的相册以读取待处理图片及保存处理结果。我们不会扫描相册中的其他内容。

We need access to your photo library to read images for processing and to save the results. We do not scan other content in your album.
